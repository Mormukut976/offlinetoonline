# 🎓 Founder Learning & Research Roadmap

Skills that will make you an unstoppable 1-person agency founder. No academic theory — only high-leverage practical knowledge.

---

## 1. Client Psychology & Sales Pitching (High Priority)
* **The Reality**: Indian local business owners do not care about "clean code" or "JavaScript". They only care about:
  1. *Meri dukan / meri gaadi par naya grahak aayega kya?*
  2. *Mahine ka koi extra kharcha toh nahi hoga?*
  3. *Mera competitor mujhse aage toh nahi nikal raha?*
* **What to practice**:
  * Never pitch features (e.g. "We use Schema markup").
  * Always pitch outcomes: *"Bhaiya Google par jab koi search karega 'Innova taxi Jaipur', toh aapka office 5-star rating ke saath sabse upar dikhega aur seedha aapka WhatsApp khulega."*
  * Always show live proof: *"Yeh dekhiye Bhumika Tour & Travels ki live website aur Google Maps listing jo humne banayi hai."*

---

## 2. Google Business Profile & Local SEO Mechanics
* **How Google Maps Local 3-Pack Ranks**:
  1. **Relevance**: Exact category (e.g. "Taxi service" vs "Transportation"), keyword in description, listed services.
  2. **Prominence**: Number of 5-star reviews containing keywords (e.g. *"Great Innova trip to Khatu Shyam"*), verified phone number, weekly photos.
  3. **Proximity**: Address pin accuracy.
* **Topic to research**:
  * Watch 2-3 YouTube videos on: *"How to rank in Google Maps Local 3-Pack for local businesses"*.

---

## 3. Serverless Form-to-Google Sheets Automation
* **Goal**: When a customer fills an enquiry form on any client's website, that data should automatically appear in the client's Google Sheet (Excel) and send an SMS/WhatsApp alert — at **₹0 server cost**.
* **Topics to explore**:
  * **Google Apps Script** (Free script inside Google Sheets that receives web requests).
  * **SheetDB** / **Formspree** (Free form endpoints).

---

## 4. Google Call-Only Ads (Instant Leads Machine)
* **Why it matters**: Local service clients (plumbers, taxis, packers & movers, clinic doctors) love **direct phone calls**.
* **What to research**:
  * How to set up a simple **Google Search "Call-Only" Campaign** with a ₹200 - ₹300 daily budget.
  * Negative keywords (to avoid wasting money on irrelevant clicks like "free taxi job in jaipur").

---

## 5. Domain, DNS & Cloud Edge Infrastructure (You Already Know 70%!)
* **What you already mastered today**:
  * Namecheap domain procurement.
  * A-Records (`75.2.60.5`) & CNAME (`www`).
  * TXT Verification records for Google Search Console.
  * Netlify Global CDN deployment & SSL provisioning.
* **Next thing to observe**:
  * How DNS propagation takes 5-15 minutes worldwide and how to check it via tools like `whatsmydns.net`.
